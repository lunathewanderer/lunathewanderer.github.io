there is something indescribably heartfelt about those sleepovers.

to begin with, uni life has stripped me of the familiarity that i once had. it's not necessarily a bad thing tbh, i have met new like-minded friends and have had some novel experiences. such stuff should make me feel electrified enough, i suppose. yet, a part of me still feels unhealthily empty somehow. i dread the subtle loneliness i feel all the time and my tendency to have intrusive thoughts every now and then.

and i haven't met my old friends in a while too, ever since i study away from home. now that i can finally reunite with them, i feel oddly at home. for once, i can worry about nothing else in this world, just live in the moment, sit back and relax and game or sing along together. and we rambled on and on about the life back then and the live we are having, the road to our school, the new friends we made, the discontent and the joy at the new place. we shared the same homesickness, we talked about the future and the dreams, and yapped about random topics like how the districts in hcmc differ =)), so on and so forth. tbf, we can do nothing, just share a bed and be together is more than enough to make me feel happy. 

and for once, i feel so reciprocal and requited, so relatable and included, again. feel like i've found a cure for the chronic loneliness and homesickness i'm having rn. just forget everything that make me feel so drowned, just chill out knowing that i still belong to somewhere in the world, isn't that lovely and simple?

sleepovers are heartfelt, in that the nights are both ever-lasting and ephemeral,

and everything is gonna be okay, i guess.

thanks win, pi, nle for the unforgettable night. and thanks punko for the ride the trip and the yap.
