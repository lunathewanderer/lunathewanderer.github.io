My life at my hometown is lovely and peaceful, in that I got to see the faces of my beloved family and friends on a daily basis. In every evening, I would find myself at the coffee shop with my friends, chilling ourselves out. Sometimes we would even go out for "bánh ép", or whatever unhealthy-but-immediately-satiate-our-appetite-and-impulse-kinda-snacks we find on the road. We would also paint the town red, hit the Cinestar, window-shop at Aeon Mall, have the best meal ever at Texas Chicken, indulge in the glamour at Fahasa, and only go back home at half past ten. On other days, I would go home, having the fresh homemade food with my family, then tuck myself in the warmth of my bed. Or we would eat out at a nice, cozy Japanese restaurant together. And the list goes on and on.

To be honest, I haven't mentally prepared to relinquish that peaceful life at all. I still long for those surreal experience again, and again, every single day. *"You don't know what you've got until it's gone"*, they say. But I know it only too well, that life is ephemeral. I know, those halcyon days will one day recede into the mists of time. I know, but I can't help myself but feel nostalgic, and sad, whenever I think about that time. And I think about that time everyday, in fact.
