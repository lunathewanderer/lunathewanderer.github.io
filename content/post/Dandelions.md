I want to ramble on, for just a little bit.

My highschool life is lovely and peaceful, in that I got to see the faces of my beloved family and friends on a daily basis. In the evening, I would find myself at the coffee shop with `nqkisme` `anhkiet0889` chilling ourselves out. Sometimes, I would even go out for "bánh ép" on the class with `dhoai` `hoangnhatphan`, or whatever unhealthy-but-immediately-satiate-our-appetite-and-impulse-kinda-snacks we find on the road. I would also paint the town red, hit the `Cinestar`, window-shop at `Aeon Mall`, have the best meal ever at Texas Chicken or a random grill BBQs with the boys, indulge in the glamour of `Fahasa`, and only go back home at half past ten with `pinosaur`. I also miss those random memories with `punko` `juju` `lemi` `kdung` `nhanle` `klong` and especially my lifelong, fricking funny friend `niwguen`. On other days, I would go home, having the fresh homemade food with my fam, then tuck myself in the warmth of my bed (or the chill of the AC). Or we would eat out at a nice, cozy Japanese restaurant together. And back then, I feel like I have something to live for, the `NEC` dream with `mngoc` and `kietble`. And the list goes on and on.

To be honest, I haven't mentally prepared to relinquish that peaceful life at all. I still long for those surreal experience again, and again, every single day. *"You don't know what you've got until it's gone"*, they say. But I know it only too well, that life is ephemeral. I know, those halcyon days will one day recede into the mists of time. I know, but I still can't help myself but feel nostalgic and sad whenever I think about that time. And I keep reminiscing about that time all the time.

Life at HCMC is not all glitz and glam as I once thought. It is somewhat overwhelming, having dinner all by myself, walking back to my dormitory alone, and studying in solitude. I feel lonely, even though I should not feel so. I just can not shrug that feeling off somehow. 

I have made some new friends, kid you not. My juniors `Ahrial`, `VuiVe`, `mdk`, `vtnam207`, `devobass` are very supportive, my friends are cheerful, aspiring, and acquisitive. I also get close to an old friend of mine `Drakie14`, an online friend on Discord `lun`, and many more, like `Bakugou Katsuki`, `EternalSnooper`.

And so, I have a friend group, we went to the city together, we hang out frequently, we help each other in our study, and together we find ourselves stand side by side in the academic battles. We stayed up late for `NSUCrypto` and we came so close to `Nanjing`. I can feel the camaraderie solacing my soul bit by bit. Probably, one day, we will set our foot on a faraway land, together.
