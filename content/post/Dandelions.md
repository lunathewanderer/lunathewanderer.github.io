Life is ephemeral. I know that very well. Still, I can't help but feel a bit sad and nostalgic whenever I think about that.

*"You don't know what you've got until it's gone"*

My highschool life at my hometown is lovely and peaceful, in that I got to see the faces of my beloved family and friends on a daily basis. In every evening, I would find myself at the coffee shop with my friends, chilling ourselves out. Sometimes we would even go out for "bánh ép", or whatever unhealthy-but-satiate-our-appetite snacks we find on the road. On other days, I would go home, having the fresh homemade food with my family. Or we would eat out at a nice, cozy Japanese restaurant together.
